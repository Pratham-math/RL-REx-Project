What the traces show

The extension matched REx

Across all five tasks, the two policies selected the same parents and generated identical code sequences: 34 generations each. Actual token totals also match.

The modified policy received two positive partial-progress updates. Those updates and the cost normalization did not change the selected trajectories here.

How far this result goes

  • Five development problems and one seed.
  • Three to six repair tests, five hidden tests per problem.
  • No separate progress-only or cost-only runs.
  • The larger held-out study has not been run.

Task-bootstrap intervals are descriptive at this sample size. An identical observed outcome and a zero-width paired interval do not establish population equivalence.
